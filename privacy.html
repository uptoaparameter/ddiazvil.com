Who is responsible

David Díaz-Villarejo operates this personal academic website. For privacy questions, email ddiazvil@eco.uc3m.es.

Google Analytics

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website traffic and improve the site. Google Analytics is not loaded when you reject analytics or before you make a choice.

The service may process page URLs and titles, referrers and traffic sources, session statistics, approximate location, browser and device information, language, scroll activity, and outbound-link clicks. Do not submit personal information through URL parameters.

Legal basis and recipient

The legal basis is your consent. Analytics data is sent to and processed by Google Ireland Limited and its service providers under Google's applicable data-processing terms. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
